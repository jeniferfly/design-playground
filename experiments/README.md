# 🧪 Creative Experiments

> Explore boldly. Learn continuously. Build intentionally.

This directory is home to independent creative experiments within Design Playground. It brings together design, storytelling, interactive experiences, emerging technology, and AI-assisted creative development.

Each experiment is an opportunity to explore an idea, test a creative direction, develop new skills, and turn curiosity into something tangible.

## Creative Approach

- Start with a meaningful creative idea or question.
- Explore alternative directions and establish a clear visual foundation.
- Prototype, test, and refine through iterative development.
- Use AI to support exploration and execution while keeping creative decisions human-led.
- Document meaningful decisions, lessons learned, and validated outcomes.

## Organization

Each experiment has its own directory and README. Depending on the project, it may include concept documentation, selected visual assets, playable prototypes, source code, and curated technical documentation.

Not every experiment needs to become a finished product. Some become portfolio pieces, others become reusable patterns, and others simply help inform future creative work.

## Documentation Philosophy

Curate, don't accumulate. Preserve meaningful milestones and approved work rather than every draft or intermediate artifact. Clearly distinguish concept imagery, working prototypes, and validated releases.
